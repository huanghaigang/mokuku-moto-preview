Built for the hard parts of the road

Phone navigation relies on satellites alone, so it drifts and jumps where the signal is poor. MOKUKU MOTO adds its own motion sensors to hold a steady position.

Satellites: reflected Position on course

  1. 1 · Downtown

    Between tall buildings

    Glass and concrete bounce satellite signals around. The inertial sensors keep your position from jumping across the street.

  2. 2 · Rush hour

    Slow traffic, steady arrow

    At walking pace and at the lights, where satellite heading wanders, the magnetometer keeps the arrow pointing the right way.

  3. 3 · Tunnel

    Into the tunnel. Still on course.

    When the sky disappears, the inertial sensors carry on, tracking up to 400 m without satellite signal.2

  4. 4 · The pass

    Turn after turn

    Through switchbacks and junctions one after another, the IMU senses each lean and change of direction as it happens.

  5. 5 · Off grid

    Beyond phone coverage

    Download maps in the app before you go. Where there's no phone signal, positioning carries on from satellites and the device's own sensors.5
